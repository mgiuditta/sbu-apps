Export a chat from your messaging app and Sbu Chats turns it into numbers, on your iPhone: how many messages you've sent each other, who replies faster, what time you talk.

- Imports exported chats as .zip, with or without media, or .txt, from the share sheet or Files
- Reads iPhone and Android exports, in English, Italian and other languages, with 12- or 24-hour times
- The total of messages, then for each person: messages, words, photos and videos, conversations started and ended, longest streak
- Typical and average reply time for each person
- Charts by hour, weekday, month and year, your messages apart from everyone else's
- The most used words and emoji, for everyone or one person
- Search every message, exact phrases too
- Period: the whole chat, last 30 days, 6 months, a year, or the dates you choose
- Export the same chat again and Sbu Chats adds only the new messages
- If a file can't be read, it tells you why and what to do
- Every stat free, with no limit on chats or analyses per day
- No internet connection: chats stay on your iPhone and you delete them whenever you want
- A sample chat to try right away
- Light and dark, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver

Sbu does one thing: it turns an exported chat into its numbers, on your iPhone.

No subscription. No in-app purchases. No account. No ads. No tracking. Data stays on your device.
