Sbu Find listens to the Bluetooth around you and guides you to the headphones, earbud or device you lost: the closer you get, the higher the percentage.

- Nearby devices with name, type and a ring showing signal strength
- Hot and cold search: a filtered percentage that doesn't jump, with a word next to it, from Cold to Very close
- A tick that speeds up and a vibration as you get closer, so you search without looking at the screen
- Your devices at the top of the list, recognized by name too when iOS changes their identifier
- Play a sound in headphones still connected to your iPhone, starting soft and growing
- Notify me if I lose it: a notification when you stop hearing one of your devices
- On the map, where your iPhone was when it last heard it
- The search on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
- A step-by-step guide to find a single earbud
- Help that says plainly what Sbu Find cannot hear, and when to use Apple's Find My
- Light and dark, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver

Sbu Find only hears Bluetooth Low Energy devices that are on and out of their case, and it measures signal strength, not distance.

Sbu does one thing: it helps you find a Bluetooth device nearby.

No subscription. No in-app purchases. No account. No ads. No tracking. Data stays on your device.
