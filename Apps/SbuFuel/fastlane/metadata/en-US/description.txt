Log each fuel-up with the odometer, the quantity and what you paid: at the top you see your vehicle's average consumption, worked out from full tank to full tank.

- Full-to-full consumption: partial fills add to the stretch, a missed fuel-up never skews the average
- Entries in any order: add a forgotten receipt or years of history, consumption is worked out again
- Odometer check: it names the entry a reading doesn't fit with
- mpg (US), mpg (UK), L/100 km and km/L; gallons or liters; miles or kilometers per vehicle
- Several vehicles: car, motorcycle, van, with optional plate and VIN
- Spending this month and this year, fuel cost per mile, average price per gallon
- Charts of consumption, monthly spending and fuel price, over 3 months, 12 months or all
- Maintenance: oil change, tires, brakes, inspection, insurance, road tax or anything you name
- Reminders by distance, by months or both, with a notification on the day it's due
- Export as CSV and import a CSV, from other services too, picking the columns
- Sample data to try it right away
- Light and dark, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver

Sbu does one thing: it tells you how much fuel your vehicle uses, from full tank to full tank.
