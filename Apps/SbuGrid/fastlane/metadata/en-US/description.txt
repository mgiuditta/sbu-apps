Choose a photo and Sbu Grid lays a grid over it: you copy it onto paper one cell at a time, with the right proportions.

- Free columns and rows, or square cells: the rows always say how many they are and why
- Paper format: A5, A4, A3, Letter or custom, portrait or landscape
- Cells in centimeters or inches: rows and columns come from the sheet
- Crop the photo to the sheet's shape, with handles you reach even on iPhone SE
- Diagonals and row and column numbers
- Dark or light line, thickness and opacity
- Black and white to read light and shade
- Zoom and full screen to look at a cell up close
- Trace: the photo see-through with the grid over the camera, to trace onto paper with your iPhone on a stand
- Save to Photos or share at the photo's resolution, and a PDF at real scale to print
- Choose the photo without giving access to your library
- Light and dark, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver

Sbu Grid helps you draw on paper: it has no brushes and no drawing on the screen.

Sbu does one thing: it lays a grid over the photo you want to draw.

No subscription. No in-app purchases. No account. No ads. No tracking. Data stays on your device.
