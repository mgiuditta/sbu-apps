No account. Sbu Grid has no server and makes no network calls: no analytics, ads or tracking. Data stays on the device.

To review:
1. Tap "Scegli una foto" (Choose a photo) and pick any photo. The Photos picker runs outside the app: Sbu Grid sees only that photo and asks for no library permission.
2. The grid lies over the photo. "Grid" changes columns, rows, square cells, diagonals, numbers, line color, thickness, opacity and black and white.
3. "Sheet" picks a paper format (A4, Letter...) and a cell size in cm or inches, and crops the photo to the sheet's shape.
4. "Trace" shows the photo see-through over the rear camera, to trace onto paper with the iPhone on a stand. The camera only feeds a preview: nothing is recorded or saved.
5. The share button at the top right exports the gridded photo to Photos (add-only permission), shares it, or shares a PDF at real scale.

How it differs from the other Sbu apps: it is the only one that helps draw from a photo, with a grid over it.
