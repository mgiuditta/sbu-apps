Data stays on your device.

Add the habits you want to build and hold one down when you do it. At the top you see your full streak: the days in a row you did everything.

- Up to 24 habits, on four pages
- Ready-made templates in five categories, or a habit all your own
- Yes or no, or several times a day: 8 glasses of water
- Every day, on chosen days, N times a week or a month, or one day only
- Timed habits: start the timer from the ring and the habit marks itself done at the end; the countdown stays on the Lock Screen
- Habits to avoid with a daily limit: when you give in, hold it down
- Link a habit to Health: steps, water, sleep, Activity rings, distances, exercise and mindful minutes, and more
- It marks itself done when you reach the daily goal, even with the app closed
- Health is read only: Sbu Habits never writes to it and your Health data stays on your iPhone
- A note for each day, to remember how it went
- Skip today when you are ill, without losing your streak
- Start of the day for night shifts
- Reminders only on due days and only if you have not done it yet, also at the time you usually do it
- Interactive widgets: mark from the Home Screen
- Widgets for a habit's streak, completion, weekdays and calendar
- Apple Watch: mark habits from your wrist with a tap, see your full streak and add the complication to your watch face. Your Watch gets its data only from your iPhone
- Statistics with your best streak, completion over time and history, to share as an image
- Export and import your data as a file, saved where you choose
- iCloud stays off until you turn it on: then your habits sync across your devices, through your own iCloud
- Share a habit with anyone you like, through iCloud and only when you choose: they see it read only
- Light and dark mode, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver

Sbu does one thing: it counts the days in a row you keep a habit.
