No account needed. All data stays on the device. No network calls.

Sbu Habits counts the days in a row you keep your habits. The app opens on a single screen with an empty grid.

1. Add a habit: tap "Add", enter a title, choose how many times a day, the schedule (every day, chosen weekdays or N times a week) and a symbol, then tap "Add" in the top right corner.
2. Mark a habit as done: touch and hold its ring on the main screen for half a second. The ring fills and the "Full streak" figure at the top updates. Hold again on a habit already done today to take the last mark back.
3. Tap a habit once (no hold) to open its menu: Undo last, Skip today (keeps the streak when you are ill), Edit.
4. "Statistics" lists each habit's streak; tap a habit there to see its best streak and history.

Reminders: in the habit form, "Add reminder" adds a reminder time. iOS asks for notification permission only then, when the first reminder is added, never at launch. Reminders fire only on due days and only if the habit is not done yet.

Interactive widget: add the "Today's habits" (small, medium, large) or "One habit" (small) widget from the Home Screen. Tapping a habit in the widget marks it done without opening the app.

Habits to avoid: in the habit form, set "Kind" to "To avoid" and choose a "Daily limit" (0 for "no smoking", 2 for "at most two coffees"). Each time you give in, touch and hold the habit (VoiceOver users use the "I gave in" action). The day counts as done if you stay within the limit when the day ends; going over the limit breaks the streak at once. The widgets show habits to avoid too.

Apple Health (read only): in the form of a habit to do, turn on "Link to Health", choose a measure (Steps, Water, Mindful Minutes, Walking + Running Distance, Active Energy) and a daily goal, then save. iOS asks for Health read access only then, never at launch. To try it on a device: link a habit to Steps with a low goal (for example 100), open the Health app, Browse > Activity > Steps > Add Data, enter a value above the goal, then reopen Sbu Habits: the habit is marked done for today. It also marks itself in the background while the app is closed. Health only adds a mark: it never removes one, and the habit can still be marked by hand. If access is denied, the user marks it by hand. Sbu Habits never writes to Health, does not store Health values, and sends nothing off the device. The Health capability is used only for this feature.

Export and import: Settings > Data. "Export" creates a JSON file with all habits and their history and opens the share sheet, so the user chooses where to save it (for example Save to Files). "Import" opens the file picker; after choosing a file made with Export, the app shows what the file contains and asks to confirm "Replace current data" (destructive) before replacing anything. No network is involved.

Start of the day: the gear in the top right corner opens Settings, where "Start of the day" sets the hour the day begins, for people who work night shifts. What you do before that hour counts for the day before. Settings also link to the privacy policy on sbu.dev.

VoiceOver users mark a habit with the "Mark as done" action instead of the hold.

Shared habits (iCloud, optional): in the form of an existing habit, "Share" opens the system iCloud sharing screen to invite people, read only. The habit goes to a zone of the owner's own iCloud (CloudKit private database), never to a server of ours; reminders, notes and Health details are not shared. Invited people need Sbu Habits and iCloud: the habit appears on their grid with a person badge, read only, and opens a screen with its statistics, where "Remove" leaves the share. The app registers for remote notifications only to receive iCloud's silent change notifications for shared habits (no alerts are ever shown). Without iCloud, "Share" is disabled with the reason.
