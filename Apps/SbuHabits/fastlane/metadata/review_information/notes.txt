No account. Sbu Habits has no server of its own and sends nothing to sbu.dev: no analytics, ads or tracking. By default all data stays on the device. Two optional features use the user's own iCloud, both off until the user acts: "Sync with iCloud" and "Share" on one habit. Apple Health is read only and its values never leave the device.

The app opens on one screen with an empty grid.

1. Add a habit: tap "Add" (the ring with a plus). A sheet opens with five tabs of templates (General, Health, Food, Timed, To avoid), a "Custom" row in each, and a search button. Pick one: the form opens filled in (Type, Goal, Days, Notifications, Health). Tap "Add habit" at the bottom.
2. Mark a habit done: touch and hold its ring for half a second. The ring fills and the "Full streak" figure at the top updates. Hold again to take the last mark back. VoiceOver: the "Mark as done" action.
3. Tap a habit once to open its menu: Undo last mark, Skip today (keeps the streak when you are ill), Write note, Edit. Edit has "Delete" at the bottom, with a confirmation that names the habit.
4. The star button opens "Statistics": completion, best streak and history, with a share button that exports the page as an image.

Notifications: iOS asks only when a habit needs them (a reminder, a timed habit, or a notice when Health marks a habit), never at launch. Reminders fire only on due days and only if the habit is not done yet.

Widgets: "Today's habits" and "One habit" are interactive: tapping a habit marks it done without opening the app. Streak, Completion, Days of the week and Calendar show one habit's numbers.

Timed habits: pick "Read" in the "Timed" tab. On the grid, tap the play button on its ring: the Timer opens with Pause, Resume and "Mark as done", and the countdown shows as a Live Activity on the Lock Screen. At the end the habit is marked done.

Habits to avoid: pick a template in the "To avoid" tab and set a "Daily limit" (0 for "no smoking", 2 for "at most two coffees"). Each time you give in, hold the habit (VoiceOver: the "Mark that you gave in" action). The day is done if you stay within the limit when it ends; going over breaks the streak at once.

Apple Health (read only): pick a template in the "Health" tab, or in a habit's form open "Health", turn on "Link to Health", choose a measure and a daily goal, then save. iOS asks for read access only then. To try it: link a habit to Steps with a goal of 500, in the Health app add a Steps value above it (Browse > Activity > Steps > Add Data), reopen Sbu Habits: the habit is done for today. It also marks itself in the background. Health only adds a mark, never removes one; the habit can still be marked by hand, and is marked by hand if access is denied. Sbu Habits never writes to Health and keeps no Health values, only the done mark. The iCloud sync file and shared habits hold only the habit's settings and its done marks: no value read from Health (steps, sleep, water...) is ever stored, synced to iCloud or shared, and a shared habit does not say it is linked to Health.

Settings: the gear at the bottom of the main screen. "Start of the day" sets the hour the day begins, for night shifts. Data > "Export" creates a JSON file and opens the share sheet; "Import" opens the file picker, shows what the file contains and asks to confirm "Replace current data". It also links the privacy policy.

Sync with iCloud (off by default): Settings > Data. Habits and history go to one file in the app's container in the user's own iCloud (iCloud Drive must be on; the file does not show in Files) and merge with the user's other devices. Without iCloud the footer says why.

Shared habits: in an existing habit's form, "Share" opens the system iCloud sharing screen to invite people, read only. The habit goes to a zone of the owner's iCloud (CloudKit private database); reminders, notes and Health details are not shared. Invited people need Sbu Habits and iCloud: the habit appears on their grid with a person badge and opens a screen with its statistics, where "Remove" leaves the share. The app registers for remote notifications only for iCloud's silent change notifications (no alerts). Without iCloud, "Share" is disabled with the reason.

Apple Watch app: shows today's habits from the paired iPhone (WatchConnectivity only); tap a ring to mark it. A complication shows the full streak.
