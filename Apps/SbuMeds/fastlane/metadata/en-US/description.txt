Reminds you of the whole family's medicines on time, and keeps the history of every dose: taken, skipped or forgotten.

- Several people on one iPhone: you, your children, your parents, whoever you care for, each with their own medicines
- Today: the week on top, doses grouped by time, "Take all" for a time, even early
- Taken, Skipped or Change time for each dose, with a tap or a swipe
- Every day, chosen days, every few days, every few hours, in cycles (21 on, 7 off) or as needed
- Different amounts per time: half a tablet in the morning, one at night
- Start date and duration for a short course
- Fixed times: if you change time zone, 8:00 stays 8:00
- Notifications with Taken, Skipped and Snooze, even from the Lock Screen
- A repeat reminder if you don't mark the dose, as often as you choose, gone as soon as you mark it
- Supply counted down at each dose, with a reminder when it's time to buy more
- Full history, with no limit of days, and extra doses off schedule
- PDF report for the doctor: share of doses taken and day by day, by person and period
- Widgets for the Home Screen and the Lock Screen
- Light and dark, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver

It gives no advice on medicines, doses or interactions: you write what the doctor says.

Sbu does one thing: it reminds you which medicine to take, and when, for the whole family.

No subscription. No in-app purchases. No account. No ads. No tracking. Data stays on your device.
