Open the app and you know what the moon looks like right now: how much of it is lit, whether it is waxing or waning, when the next full moon comes.

- Illuminated percentage, one large readable figure
- The moon drawn as it looks, with its lit part and the phase's name
- Day and time of the next four phases: new moon, first quarter, full moon, last quarter
- The moon's age and its distance from Earth
- Any day from 1900 to 2100: swipe for the day before or after, or pick it from a calendar
- Worked out on your iPhone: no location, no network, no permissions
- Light and dark mode, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver

Sbu does one thing: it tells you what the moon looks like.
