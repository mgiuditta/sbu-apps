No account. Sbu Moon has no server and sends nothing: no analytics, ads or tracking. Everything is worked out on the device with Jean Meeus's algorithms, without network.

The app opens on today's moon: the illuminated percentage, the phase's name, the dates of the next four phases, the moon's age and distance.

- Swipe left or right for the day after or before, or tap the date to pick any day from 1900 to 2100.
- "Show moonrise and moonset" asks for location (When In Use) only when you tap "Use my location", or lets you pick a city from a built-in list with no network. The position is read once, to about a kilometre, saved on the device as the place, and never sent anywhere.
- The gear opens Settings: phase alerts (new moon, first quarter, full moon, last quarter) at a chosen time. iOS asks for notifications only when you turn one on.

How it differs from the other Sbu apps: Sbu Moon is the only one that computes astronomy, the moon's phase, illumination, rise and set offline; the others do unrelated things (habits, photo cleanup, sound, angles, speed). Each Sbu app has its own code for its own task; they share only a small design package.
