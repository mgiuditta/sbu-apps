No account. Sbu Noise has no server and sends nothing: no analytics, ads or tracking. All sounds are in the app (free CC0 recordings and generated noises, credited in Settings (the gear at the top right) > Sound Credits); it never streams.

The app opens on the player. Tap play: the sound loops, and keeps playing in the background and with the screen locked (background audio mode). Swipe or use the arrows for the next sound; the menu at the top left picks a group (Sounds, Favorites, Mixes, Noises, Rain, Water, Nature, Home, Travel).

- The heart adds the sound to Favorites. Manage (the grid button) hides and reorders sounds.
- Plus creates a Mix of up to five sounds, each with its own volume, placed around you in headphones.
- The clock button opens Timers: a sleep timer fades the sound out; an alarm fades a sound in at the chosen time, with Snooze. Only when you add the first alarm, iOS asks for permission for the backup: a system alarm (AlarmKit) on iOS 26 and later, Time Sensitive notifications on earlier versions. The backup rings only if the app is not running at the alarm time.
- The moon button opens the bedside clock.
- Widget and Control Center control start and stop the sound; Shortcuts and Siri can play a sound or a mix.
