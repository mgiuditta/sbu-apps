Compress your videos and free up space on your iPhone. No watermark, no daily limit, and many videos at once, for free.

- Pick one or more videos from Photos, or import them from Files
- Two settings everyone gets: frame rate (24 suggested) and dimensions (80% suggested), plus quality
- See how much they will weigh before compressing: with the suggested settings an iPhone video gets about 80% lighter
- The size of each video and the total of the selection
- Compress many videos in a row: overall percentage, "video 2 of 5", a bar for each video and Stop
- A video that fails does not stop the others; one that is already light stays as it is
- At the end: before, after and how much you saved
- Copies go to Photos and to the Compressed Videos album, with the date and place of the original
- Then choose: delete the originals (the app asks, then iOS) or keep them
- Share the copies with any app
- Advanced: maximum size in MB per video, H.264 or HEVC, convert to MP4, remove audio
- Reads H.264, HEVC, .mov, .mp4 and .m4v
- Everything on your iPhone with Apple's video encoder: no video leaves the phone
- Light and dark mode, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver

Sbu does one thing: it makes your videos lighter, on your phone.

No subscription. No in-app purchases. No account. No ads. No tracking. Data stays on your device.
