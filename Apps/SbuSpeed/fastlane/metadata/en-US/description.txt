A GPS speedometer built like a dashboard instrument: your speed in large digits, a trip log, a windshield reflection. On a bike, on a run, on a boat or in a car.

- Current speed in km/h, mph or knots, one large whole number
- Altitude and course with the compass point under the number; with knots they become SOG and COG
- Top speed, average speed, distance and time of the trip
- Trip log: every trip saved with date, time, distance, top and average speed, ready to export as CSV. No location is saved
- One-tap speed limits: US and UK limits in mph, 50, 90, 110, 130 km/h, the ones on the water in knots, or your own value
- Go over the limit and the line under the number says so, and your iPhone vibrates and plays a sound once
- HUD: the number mirrored and the screen at full brightness, to lay on the dashboard at night and read in the reflection
- The average counts only time spent moving: stops do not lower it
- The dial adapts like a car's: 40, 80, 130 or 200 mph
- In landscape, in a car mount, the number sits on the left and the trip on the right
- The screen stays on while you watch your speed
- Location is used only while the app is open and stays on your iPhone
- Light and dark mode, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver

When driving, mount your iPhone before you set off and don't touch it while moving. GPS speed is a guide: your vehicle's speedometer and the law always come first.

Sbu does one thing: it tells you how fast you are going.

No subscription. No in-app purchases. No account. No ads. No tracking. Data stays on your device.
