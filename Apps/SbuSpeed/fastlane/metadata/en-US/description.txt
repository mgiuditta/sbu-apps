Open the app and read your speed in large digits, from your iPhone's GPS. On a bike, on a run, on a boat or in a car: one number, readable from a distance.

- Current speed in km/h or mph, one large whole number
- Top speed, average speed and distance of the trip
- The average counts only time spent moving: stops do not lower it
- Reset the trip with a confirmation
- The screen stays on while you watch your speed
- Location is used only while the app is open and stays on your iPhone
- Light and dark mode, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver

Sbu does one thing: it tells you how fast you are going.
