No account. Sbu Speed has no server and sends nothing: no analytics, ads or tracking. Location is used only while the app is open (When In Use), never in the background, and no position is stored or logged.

The app opens on the speedometer and iOS asks for location. The current speed from GPS shows as one large number; under it altitude and course (in knots: SOG and COG), the dial, the trip (top speed, average while moving, distance, time) and four one-tap speed limits.

New in 1.1:
- One-tap limits under the trip (km/h 50/90/110/130, mph by region, knots 3/6/10/20); a tap on the active one turns it off. Any whole value in Settings, "Other value". Going over vibrates and plays a sound once.
- "Save and reset" keeps the trip in Trips (list icon, top left): start and end time, distance, time, top and average speed. Numbers only, no positions. Trips can be deleted and exported as CSV.
- "HUD": the number mirrored on a dark screen at full brightness, to place on the dashboard at night and read in the windshield reflection. Tap anywhere to go back.

The app needs no interaction while driving: the screen stays on and only shows the speed. Without movement the speed reads 0 and the course shows a dash; testing needs a walk or a drive. With location denied, the screen says so and offers to open Settings.

How it differs from the other Sbu apps: Sbu Speed is the only one that uses GPS, to measure speed, course and trips; the others do unrelated things (angles with the motion sensors, sound level, habits, moon phases, photo cleanup). Each Sbu app has its own code for its own instrument; they share only the design system.
