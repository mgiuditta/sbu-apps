No account. Sbu Speed has no server and sends nothing: no analytics, ads or tracking. Location is used only while the app is open (When In Use) and stays on the device.

The app opens on the speedometer and iOS asks for location. The current speed from GPS shows as one large number, with the trip below: top speed, average speed while moving, distance and time.

- "Reset trip" clears the trip after a confirmation.
- The gear opens Settings: units (km/h, mph or knots) and an optional speed limit; going over it vibrates and plays a sound once.
- In landscape, as in a car mount, the number sits on the left and the trip on the right.
Without movement the speed reads 0; testing needs a walk or a drive. With location denied, the screen says so and offers to open Settings.
