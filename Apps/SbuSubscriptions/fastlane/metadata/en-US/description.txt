Add your subscriptions and at the top you see what you spend a year, or a month. Before each renewal a reminder tells you the day and the amount.

- Unlimited subscriptions, from a list of known services or your own
- Weekly, monthly, quarterly and yearly cycles, every N days, weeks, months or years, one-off or in instalments
- What you spend in one figure, with other currencies converted at the ECB rates included in the app, or at your own
- Free trials with the days left and the price after the trial
- Reminders before each renewal and before a trial ends, at the time you choose
- Payments calendar with the month's total and what's still to pay
- Price changes from a date, payment history and Mark as paid
- Categories, lists and payment methods; sort, group, filter and search
- Pause, cancel and reactivate without losing anything
- Help to cancel: the service's website or your App Store subscriptions
- Widgets for the Home Screen and the Lock Screen
- Siri, Shortcuts and Spotlight
- Export and import as CSV, from a spreadsheet too
- iCloud stays off until you turn it on: then your subscriptions sync across your devices, through your own iCloud
- Share a list with anyone, through iCloud and only when you choose: they see it read only, with its total
- Light and dark, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver

Sbu does one thing: it tells you what your subscriptions cost, so no renewal takes you by surprise.

No subscription. No in-app purchases. No account. No ads. No tracking. Data stays on your device.
