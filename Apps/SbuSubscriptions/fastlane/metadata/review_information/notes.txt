No account. Sbu Subscriptions has no server of its own and sends nothing to sbu.dev: no analytics, ads or tracking. By default all data stays on the device. Two optional features use the user's own iCloud, both off until the user acts: "Sync with iCloud" (Settings > Data), which mirrors the store to the user's private CloudKit database, and sharing one list read only with people they invite (Settings > Lists, the share button next to a list), through CloudKit sharing.

This is version 2.0 of an app already on the App Store, rebuilt in Swift. At first launch it brings over the subscriptions saved by version 1.0 on the same device; on a new install it opens empty.

1. Add a subscription: tap "+" at the top left, or "Add" on the empty screen. Search a known service (for example "Netflix") or tap "Custom". The form opens: type a price and tap "Save". The home shows what you spend a year, with the subscription under "Active".
2. Tap a subscription to open it: Edit, Change price, History (payments with "Mark as paid"), How to cancel (the service's website in Safari, or the App Store subscriptions sheet), Pause, Mark as cancelled, Delete (with confirmation).
3. "Payments calendar" under the figure shows the month: a dot on each payment day; touch and hold a day to add a subscription from it.
4. The menu next to "+" sorts, groups and filters the list; the total follows the filter.

Notifications: iOS asks only once a subscription has a reminder (one day before by default), never at launch. Settings > Reminders has the reminder time and "Send a test reminder".

Exchange rates: the ECB reference rates are bundled with the app and refreshed with each release; the app never goes online. Users can type their own rate in Settings > Exchange rates.

Widgets: "Upcoming payments" for the Home Screen (small, medium, large) and the Lock Screen, read-only, configurable by list.

Siri and Shortcuts: "Next renewal in Sbu Subscriptions", "How much do I pay this month with Sbu Subscriptions". Answers about money need the iPhone unlocked.

How it differs from the other Sbu apps: it is the only one about subscriptions and recurring payments; the others measure, track habits or play sounds.
