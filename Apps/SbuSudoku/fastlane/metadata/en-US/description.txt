Sbu Sudoku is classic 9 by 9 sudoku, with six difficulties from Easy to Extreme. No ads, no purchases, no waiting.

- Six difficulties: Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert, Evil, Extreme. Every puzzle has exactly one solution
- The Daily Sudoku: the same puzzle for everyone, one attempt a day
- Pencil notes that clear themselves from the row, column and box when you write a digit
- Mistakes shown at once, three mistakes end the game; the second chance is free and takes one away
- Unlimited hints: they reveal the cell you pick
- Undo up to 25 moves
- Points for every right cell: the faster you are, the more they're worth, and completed rows, columns and boxes multiply them
- A best score for each difficulty, and the full stop turns to color when you beat it
- The game saves after every move: close the app whenever you like and pick up where you left off
- Every aid can be turned off in settings. Works with VoiceOver. Light and dark, offline

Sbu does one thing: sudoku you can play without interruptions.

No subscription. No in-app purchases. No account. No ads. No tracking. Data stays on your device.
