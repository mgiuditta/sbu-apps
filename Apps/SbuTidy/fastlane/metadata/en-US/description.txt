Give each chore in your home a frequency: dirt builds up over time, and at the top you see how many things are due today. No rigid deadlines: clean what needs it, when it needs it.

- "To do" list by room, dirtiest first; a "Coming up" filter to get ahead
- Rooms with their base chores: kitchen, bathroom, bedrooms, living room, garden and more
- Suggestions for every room, base and special, or chores of your own
- Frequency in days, weeks or months, and effort from one to three
- Dirt level for each chore: "in 3 days", "today", "2 days late"
- "Done now" with a swipe, "Done on…" for a past day, history for every chore
- Pause for holidays: dirt doesn't build up until you resume
- One-off chores that disappear once done
- Duplicate a room with all its chores, reorder and rename
- Widgets for the Home Screen and the Lock Screen
- Reminders only on days when something becomes due, never empty notifications
- iPhone and iPad, landscape too
- Light and dark, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver

It works on one device: there is no sync with other people in your home.

Sbu does one thing: it tells you what to clean at home today.

No subscription. No in-app purchases. No account. No ads. No tracking. Data stays on your device.
