Cleanup helps you free up space on your iPhone: you download it free, then deleting for real asks for Cleanup Pro, weekly included. Sbu Clean does the same one thing, free and with no limit: it frees up space in your Photos library, one decision at a time.

## What Sbu Clean does

At the top you see how much space you can free and, below, your iPhone's storage. Swipe left for the bin and right to keep; undo the last choice with one tap. Go month by month, through recents, at random, On This Day or one album at a time, and pick up where you left off.

Vision finds duplicates and similar photos on your iPhone. The best one stays, chosen by face, sharpness, favourites and edits; the copies are already selected and you decide. Screenshots and screen recordings sit in a grid, to select all at once. Videos are sorted from the heaviest, and you can compress them while keeping the original until you decide. Live Photos become stills and you save the video part.

Photos you are unsure about go aside, or you keep them for good: they won't come up again. At the end you see the space freed, in total and in the last clean-up.

## What it doesn't do

It doesn't clean contacts, email or your iCloud Drive. It never deletes anything by itself and doesn't promise space that won't come back: iOS keeps deleted photos for 30 days, and the app tells you.

## Why it's free

Sbu takes the top-paid apps for simple jobs and makes a free alternative to each. Every app keeps six promises: no subscription, no in-app purchases, no account, no ads, no tracking, data only on your device. The code is public on GitHub.
