My Moon Phase Pro is a paid lunar calendar app: it costs 3,99 €. Sbu Moon does the same one thing, free: open the app and you know what the moon looks like now.

## What Sbu Moon does

In large type you see the percentage lit, and beside it the moon drawn as it is, with the phase name and whether it is waxing or waning. Below are the day and time of the next four phases: new moon, first quarter, full moon, last quarter.

Pick the phases you want an alert for and the time to hear about them. You see when the moon rises and sets where you are, its age and its distance from Earth. You can look at any day from 1900 to 2100.

Everything is worked out on your iPhone, with no network. Your location is used only for moonrise and moonset, if you ask, and stays on your iPhone.

## What it doesn't do

It has no weather and no golden or blue hours for photography: an app that never goes online can't know if it will be cloudy.
