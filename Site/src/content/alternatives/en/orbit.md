Orbit is a subscription tracker: you download it free, then many features ask for Orbit Plus, which you pay for. Sbu Subscriptions does the same one thing, all free: it tells you what you spend on subscriptions and reminds you before every renewal.

## What Sbu Subscriptions does

Add your subscriptions and one figure sits at the top: what you spend a year, or a month. Other currencies are converted at the ECB reference rates included in the app.

Before every renewal you get a reminder with the day and the amount, at the time you choose. For free trials you see the days left and what you will pay after.

The calendar shows this month's payments and what is left to pay. You can pause, cancel and resume a subscription without losing its history. When you want to cancel, the app takes you to the service's site or to your App Store subscriptions.

There are also Home and Lock Screen widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, read-only shared lists through iCloud, and CSV export and import.

## What it doesn't do

It doesn't read screenshots, bank statements or emails to find your subscriptions by itself. You add them, from a list of known services, or import them from a CSV. That is a choice: an app that never reads your banking documents can't misuse them.

It doesn't connect to your bank and has no account. If you need all that, Orbit may suit you.

## Why it's free

Sbu takes simple jobs your iPhone can already do and makes a free app for each one. Every app keeps six promises: no subscription, no in-app purchases, no account, no ads, no tracking, data only on your device. The code is public on GitHub.
