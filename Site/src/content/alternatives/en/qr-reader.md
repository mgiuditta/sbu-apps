QR Reader for iPhone, by TapMedia, is now called QR Code ϟ. It downloads free, shows ads and offers a paid PRO version. Sbu Scanner does the one thing you need: it reads any code and shows you where it goes before you open it, with no ads.

## What Sbu Scanner does

Point at a code and it reads itself: QR, Data Matrix, Aztec, PDF417 and the barcodes EAN, UPC, Code 128, Code 39 and more. You get the torch, the front camera and pinch to zoom. It also reads a code from a photo, without asking for access to your library.

Before you open a link you see the site's name in large type and the full address. The app warns you when the link uses http, a numeric address, letters that imitate other letters, or a link shortener. It's the difference between opening a QR code on a parking meter and trusting one.

With a Wi-Fi QR code it joins the network or copies the password. Contacts, events, email, phone, SMS and locations open or get added with one tap.

The history has search, favourites and folders, and exports to CSV. You can also make QR codes and barcodes and export them as PNG or SVG.

## What it doesn't do

It doesn't scan PDF documents: your iPhone's Notes app does that. It doesn't look up prices, deals or ingredients online: it gives you the barcode number and you choose where to search.

## Why it's free

Sbu takes simple jobs your iPhone can already do and makes a free app for each one. Every app keeps six promises: no subscription, no in-app purchases, no account, no ads, no tracking, data only on your device. The code is public on GitHub.
