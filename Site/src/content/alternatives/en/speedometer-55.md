Speedometer 55 is a paid GPS speedometer: it costs 3,99 € and also does maps, video and trip costs. Sbu Speed does the one thing you need, for free: it tells you how fast you are going.

## What Sbu Speed does

You see your speed large, in km/h, mph or knots. Under the figure are altitude and heading, and the top speed, average, distance and duration of the trip. The average counts only time in motion, so stops do not pull it down.

Pick a limit with one tap, 50, 90, 110 or 130 km/h or your own: when you pass it you read it under the figure and your iPhone vibrates. At night, set your iPhone on the dashboard and read the mirrored figure in the windshield.

Every trip goes into the log with date, duration, distance, top speed and average, and you can export it as CSV. Locations are not saved.

## What it doesn't do

It has no maps, no camera video and no fuel cost. For those you need a bigger app.
