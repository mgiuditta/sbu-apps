Streaks is a paid habit app, one of the best loved on the App Store: it costs 6,99 € once. Sbu Habits does the same one thing, free: it counts the days in a row you keep a habit.

## What Sbu Habits does

Add the habits you want to build, up to 24 on four pages, from ready templates or your own, and tap and hold when you do them. At the top you see the Full streak: the days in a row you did everything.

A habit can be yes or no, or several times a day, like eight glasses of water. It can be every day, on chosen days or N times a week. Timed habits start from the ring and complete themselves at the end. Habits to avoid have a daily limit.

Linked to Health, a habit completes by itself when you reach the goal: steps, water, sleep, Activity rings, mindful minutes. Reminders come only on the planned days and only if you haven't done it yet. You can skip a sick day without losing the streak, and write a note for each day.

There are interactive widgets, the Apple Watch app with its complication, statistics to share as an image, iCloud if you turn it on, and read-only shared habits.

## What it doesn't do

It has no colour or seasonal themes: Sbu Habits is ink on paper, with colour only on the full stop. It never writes to Health.

## Why it's free

Sbu takes the top-paid apps for simple jobs and makes a free alternative to each. Every app keeps six promises: no subscription, no in-app purchases, no account, no ads, no tracking, data only on your device. The code is public on GitHub.
