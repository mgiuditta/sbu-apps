      a: Sì. Nessun abbonamento, nessun acquisto in-app, nessuna pubblicità, nessun account. Anche la ricerca, Fai suonare, l'avviso e la mappa.

      a: Yes. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads, no account. The search, Play a sound, the alert and the map too.
    - q: What can it find?
      a: Headphones, earbuds, watches, speakers and other Bluetooth Low Energy devices, if they are on and out of their case. A device that is off or flat sends nothing, and Sbu Find cannot hear it.
