A subscription takes three taps to cancel, if you know where to look. The trouble is that they don't all live in the same place.

## App Store subscriptions

These are the ones you pay with your Apple Account: apps, Apple Music, iCloud+, Apple TV+, games.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **your name** at the top.
3. Tap **Subscriptions**.

You see active and expired subscriptions, with the price and the next renewal date. Tap the one you want to end, then **Cancel Subscription**.

You can also get there from the **App Store**: tap your picture at the top right, then **Subscriptions**.

## What happens when you cancel

- You keep the service **until the end of the period you already paid for**. You lose no days by cancelling now.
- A **free trial** must be cancelled at least 24 hours before it ends, or the first payment goes through. Cancelling during a trial often ends it at once: do it on the last day.
- If you don't see **Cancel Subscription**, it is already cancelled: the date shown is when it ends.

## Subscriptions that aren't there

If you pay Netflix, Spotify, Amazon Prime, a gym or a newspaper on their website or by card, you won't find them on your iPhone. You cancel them on the service's site, in your account page. Look for **Account**, **Subscription** or **Plan**, and keep the confirmation email.

To see what you really pay for, check your bank statement for the last three months: every charge that repeats is a subscription.

## Asking Apple for a refund

If you were charged for a renewal you didn't want, you can ask for it back at **reportaproblem.apple.com** with your Apple Account. Choose **Request a refund**, the reason and the purchase. Apple decides case by case, usually within a few days.

## Never forget one again

The simplest way is to keep all your subscriptions in one place, with a reminder before each renewal.

- To see what you spend in a year, use the [subscription cost calculator](/en/abbonamenti): type your subscriptions and see the total, nothing to install.
- For a reminder before every renewal and before every trial ends, there is [Sbu Subscriptions](/en/apps/subscriptions), free, with no account and no ads. It tells you what you spend a year and, when you want to cancel, takes you to the service's site or to your App Store subscriptions.
