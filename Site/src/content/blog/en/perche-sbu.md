title: Why sbu.dev
description: Simple jobs, free apps. Why sbu.dev exists.

You look for a way to track habits and find subscriptions. These are simple jobs your phone can already do.

sbu.dev makes a free app for each of these jobs, one by one. Each Sbu app does one thing, is free and asks nothing of you: no account, no ads, no data leaving your iPhone.

The first is Sbu Habits, for habits and days in a row.
