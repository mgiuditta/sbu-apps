---
title: Why sbu.dev
description: The utilities at the top of the App Store cost money. Sbu remakes them for free, one by one.
date: 2026-10-12
---

You look for a level on your iPhone and find paid apps. You look for a way to track habits and find subscriptions. These are simple jobs your phone can already do.

sbu.dev remakes these apps, one by one. Each Sbu app does one thing, is free and asks nothing of you: no account, no ads, no data leaving your iPhone.

The first two are Sbu Level, a bubble level and clinometer, and Sbu Habits, for habits and days in a row.
