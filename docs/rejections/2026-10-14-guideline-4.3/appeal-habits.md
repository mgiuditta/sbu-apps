# Sbu Habits: appeal to the App Review Board

Send from developer.apple.com/contact/app-store → App Review → "Appeal an app rejection". App: Sbu Habits, version 1.0 (build 5). Paste the text below as is (about 420 words). Send only this appeal; Clean, Noise and Moon wait for its outcome.

---

Hello,

I am appealing the 4.3 and 4.2.6 rejection of Sbu Habits 1.0 (build 5) of 14 October 2026. On 22 October App Review answered my Resolution Center reply with the general note on 4.3, which mentions apps built with templates of apps already on the App Store. Sbu Habits was not built from a template, and I would ask the Board to look at the app itself.

What the app is. A habit tracker built around one figure, the "full streak": days in a row on which every habit due was done. It has habits to avoid with a daily limit, timed habits with a Live Activity that mark themselves at the end, habits that read Apple Health and mark themselves when the day's goal is reached, an Apple Watch app with a complication, interactive widgets, skip days, a custom start of day for night shifts, and opt-in iCloud sync and read-only sharing of one habit. It is free, with no purchases, account, ads or tracking.

Why it is not a template. I am an individual developer and wrote it myself in Swift and SwiftUI. Its own code is about 14,600 lines, and it is my only app that uses HealthKit or has an Apple Watch app. The code my apps share is one small package of mine, about 1,700 lines: colours, type, buttons and the Settings screen. It holds no habit, Health or data logic.

Why it is not a duplicate. I have no other habit or tracking app and never publish two apps in the same category. My two approved apps, Sbu Scanner QR Barcode and Sbu Subscriptions, have nothing in common with Habits beyond the visual style.

What I got wrong, and changed. On 14 October I submitted six new apps within minutes, with descriptions that opened with the same lines. That made unrelated apps look like copies of each other, and I understand why. I have rewritten every description so it opens with what that app does, and from now on I submit at most one app a week, each with review notes explaining how it differs from my other apps. I have not resubmitted any of the six while waiting for this appeal.

What I ask. A review of Sbu Habits on its own: its features, its code and its use of Health, Watch and Live Activities. I can send a screen recording on a physical iPhone and Apple Watch, and I would welcome a phone call with App Review if that helps.

Thank you for your time.

Matteo Giuditta
