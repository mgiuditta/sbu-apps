########## Sbu Moon ##########

===
Thank you for the review. I'm not resubmitting Sbu Moon because I believe it meets 4.3 and 4.2.6. Answers below.

Moonrise and moonset come either from your position, read once to about a kilometre (When In Use, and only after you tap "Use my location"), or from a built-in list of cities, shown in that city's own time when it differs from the phone's.
