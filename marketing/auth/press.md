## MacStories (EN)

Subject: A free, zero-network authenticator from a security student, after a $9.99/week one
> Hi, I'm Matteo Giuditta, a Computer Security student in Milan.
> A popular iOS authenticator lists weekly in-app prices up to $9.99 and beyond, about $519 a year for TOTP codes.
> I rebuilt it as Sbu Auth: free, no in-app purchases, no account, and no network access at all.
> Secrets stay in the device Keychain; backup is an AES-GCM file you keep, and it imports Google Authenticator exports.
> The code is open: https://github.com/mgiuditta/sbu-apps
> It found an audience here: [HN/Reddit thread link].
> Three screenshots attached; app and privacy page: https://sbu.dev/apps/auth
> Thanks for reading, Matteo
